NUTRITION STRATEGY

Corrections for a Lean-Muscular Goal

The goal is not to eat as little as possible. The goal is to lose fat while preserving strength and muscle.

🥩

More Protein

Whey, eggs, soya, curd, milk and chicken help distribute protein throughout the day and support muscle retention.

🥦

More Fiber

Vegetables, oats, fruit and chia provide fiber and help make a calorie-controlled diet easier to maintain.

🥛

Better Recovery

Curd and milk provide convenient protein and nutrients while keeping the overall plan practical.

Adjust portions using your longer-term weight trend.

04

Stay Consistent

A sustainable diet beats a perfect diet for a few days.

💡

Remember

Rice at dinner is okay. Total daily calories, protein intake and your longer-term weight trend matter more than avoiding carbohydrates at night.
